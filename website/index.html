Get TinyMe
Scroll at your own pace. The phone follows you, forwards and back.
01 · Talk, and it's logged

No forms. Just say what happened.

Hold the mic and talk. Meals, workouts, moods, spending, the people you met: TinyMe picks out the numbers, links the people and files it, the way you'd tell a friend.

02 · Trackers that build themselves

Ask for a tracker. Get one.

A calories card, a spend tracker, a gym streak. TinyMe designs the card from what you've already logged and shows you a preview right in the chat. One tap puts it on Home.

03 · Your Home screen

Every card you asked for, on one screen.

Home is where your cards live: your journal, calories, gym, spending. Switch between Day, Week, Month and Year and every card recounts. Tap a card and the entries behind it slide in. Drag to reorder, and keep the numbers on a Home Screen widget too.

04 · Your week, ahead

Plans in one sentence.

Lunches, tasks, birthdays and repeating habits land on Upcoming, your week at a glance. Log the workout and the gym plan ticks itself off.

05 · Ask anything about your life

Answers from your records, not guesses.

Every answer is counted and summed from what you've logged, and the people you mention get their own profile and photos. If there's no record, TinyMe says so instead of making one up.

06 · Apple Health, built in

What your phone measures, it already knows.

Pick what to share and steps, sleep, workouts and more sync in by themselves. Ask how you slept and the answer comes from Health; a Watch workout closes the gym plan.

07 · Yours to keep

Take it with you. Or wipe it.

Everything lives on your own server. Export it all in one file, or delete everything, including what the agent remembers.

More in the app

One place for the people, places and plans in your life.

People and photos

Everyone you mention gets a profile and a memory. Share a photo in chat, say who it is, and it lands on their page.

Places, with nearby alerts

Save the café a friend mentioned. See your places on a map, and get a nudge when you walk past one you wanted to try.

Upcoming

Plans, tasks, birthdays and repeating habits on one calendar. Move or skip a single day without touching the rest.

Gentle nudges

When your Health data says you slept badly or skipped a walk, TinyMe mentions it, kindly. Turn nudges off any time.

Siri, Shortcuts and a widget

Say "Journal something" to Siri, use Back Tap, or glance at your cards on the Home Screen widget.

Works offline

No signal? Keep talking. Messages queue on the phone and send the moment you are back online.

Why it matters

Your life is spread across a dozen apps, and none of them listen.

Logging is a chore

Every tracker wants its own forms and taps. Most people stop after a week, so the record never builds up.

Nothing connects

Your calendar doesn't know you skipped the gym. Your food log doesn't know dinner was with a friend.

Assistants forget

Chatbots answer once and move on. There's no record you can count on, so "how often did I…?" has no answer.

Private by design

A diary only works if you trust where it lives.

TinyMe runs on its owner's own server. Your life log never sits in a shared database with anyone else's.

Your own server

One owner per install. The phone signs in to your server, nobody else's.

Take it with you

Export everything, including what the agent remembers, as one file you own.

Delete means delete

One action wipes the records and the agent's memory too, not just the app's view.

Encrypted backups Soon

Nightly backups, encrypted before they leave the server, and the app shows when the last one ran.

Why now

For the first time, an assistant can keep a real record of your life.

Agents can act

Today's models reliably use tools: they log, schedule and query, instead of only chatting.

Voice is effortless

Speech recognition on the phone is fast and accurate enough that talking beats tapping.

Your phone already measures you

Steps, sleep and workouts are recorded anyway. TinyMe turns them into one story with everything you say.
